GardenPro Account & Data Deletion

GardenPro allows users to delete their account and associated app data.

How to delete your account

  1. Open GardenPro and sign in.
  2. Open your Profile.
  3. Use the account deletion option and confirm the deletion.

Account deletion removes the user's GardenPro account and associated GardenPro data stored for that account.

Data deletion requests

If you need help with deletion, contact the GardenPro developer through the contact details shown on the Google Play listing.

Data retained

GardenPro does not intentionally retain the user's GardenPro account data after an account deletion request is completed, except where retention is required by law.
