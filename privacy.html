GardenPro Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 September 2026

GardenPro is a business management app for gardening professionals. This policy explains what information GardenPro stores and how it is used.

Information we store

When you create an account, GardenPro stores your account email and the business information you choose to enter, such as customer names, addresses, phone numbers, email addresses, jobs, quotes, invoices and expenses.

How we use information

Your information is used to provide the GardenPro features you request, including managing customers, scheduling jobs, preparing quotes and invoices, and recording business income and expenses.

Where information is stored

GardenPro uses Supabase for authentication and cloud database storage. Data is protected with account-based access controls so that signed-in users can access only their own GardenPro records.

Sharing

GardenPro does not sell your business data. Information is not shared with other users except when you choose to share or print information yourself, for example by giving a customer a quote or invoice.

Security

GardenPro uses authenticated connections and row-level access controls. No internet service can guarantee absolute security, so you should use a strong password and keep your account credentials private.

Account deletion

You can request permanent deletion from inside the GardenPro app using My GardenPro → Delete my account. Deleting your account permanently removes your GardenPro account and associated business records.

Your choices

You may stop using GardenPro at any time. You can also request access, correction or deletion of your information through the support contact published with the GardenPro Google Play listing.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when GardenPro's features or legal requirements change. The latest version will be published with the app.
